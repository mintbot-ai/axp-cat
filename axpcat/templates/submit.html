Before you submit

  • Run axp validate agent-extension.json and axp verify agent-extension.json locally; the gate runs the same checks.
  • Serve the manifest from https://<publisher>/.well-known/agent-extension.json, or advertise it with <link rel="agent-extension" href="..."> on your landing page.
  • Publishing on GitHub? Attach agent-extension.json to the release and list your signing key at https://<publisher>/.well-known/agent-extension-keys.json (axp keydir writes it). axp resolve https://github.com/you/my-ext shows what the gate will see.
  • The locator you submit is what visitors are told to install, so submit the one you want them to use.
